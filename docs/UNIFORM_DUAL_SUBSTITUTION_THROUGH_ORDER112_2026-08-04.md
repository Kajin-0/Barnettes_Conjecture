## Structural reduction

Restore the removed degree-12 dual vertex as an apex of color 0. The resulting graph is an even sphere triangulation and has a proper three-coloring. The twelve boundary vertices alternate colors 1 and 2.

For k inserted disk vertices, the augmented triangulation has N=13+k vertices and 2N-4=22+2k triangular faces. Every face is rainbow. Counting one edge of each color pair per face and two incident faces per edge gives

```text
E01 = E02 = E12 = 11 + k
```

Therefore the color-1/color-2 graph H12 consists of the fixed 12-cycle plus exactly k-1 extra edges.

Each internal color-0 vertex has a simple alternating link cycle in H12. Across these link cycles:

- every boundary edge occurs exactly once;
- every extra H12 edge occurs exactly twice.

Conversely, a weighted cycle decomposition is accepted exactly when its rainbow triangles form a connected closed complex with Euler characteristic two and every vertex link is one cycle. This is an exact sphere certificate and avoids generation of parity-invalid planar disks.

## Interpretation

This is a substantial bounded nonexistence result for the candidate-489 single-face dual-substitution mechanism. It is not a counterexample to Barnette’s conjecture.

Any counterexample obtained from this source by replacing the distinguished degree-12 dual vertex must use at least eight inserted disk vertices, corresponding to primal order at least 114, or must leave this one-face substitution framework.

The result also changes the strategic assessment: blind low-order growth around this face has now produced 46,672 valid closure constructions through order 112, all Hamiltonian. The next credible route should optimize the exact two-tree boundary language or use multi-face/multi-pole geometry rather than merely increasing disk order.
